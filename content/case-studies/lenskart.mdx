<CaseStudyH2>Asia's Largest Eyewear Brand</CaseStudyH2>

<CaseStudyP>

Lenskart is India's most recognised eyewear brand, serving over 40 million customers across India, Singapore, Japan, and the Middle East. With 2,000+ retail touchpoints and a strong D2C channel, they've built their edge on technology and scale.

But at 5,000+ frames and growing, keeping visual content fresh, diverse, and ad-ready across every audience segment had become an operational ceiling.

<CaseStudyH2>The Walls They Hit in D2C</CaseStudyH2>

<CaseStudyLead>Selling glasses online is a visual problem. Customers need to see the product on someone who looks like them - at a scale traditional production simply can't match.</CaseStudyLead>

<WallsGrid>

<WallCard title="Photoshoots Don't Scale">Studios, models, and photographers for 5,000+ SKUs across multiple demographics cost a lot - and still left coverage gaps.</WallCard>

<WallCard title="Slow Time-to-Market">New launches waited weeks for shoot slots. By the time assets were ready, campaign momentum was lost.</WallCard>

<WallCard title="Catalog Images Not Ad-Ready">Raw product images had inconsistent crops, cluttered backgrounds, and wrong formats - requiring a manual design pass on thousands of SKUs.</WallCard>

<WallCard title="Ad Spend on Dead SKUs">Catalog ads promoted low-stock, low-converting products with no visibility into the drain - silently eroding ROAS.</WallCard>
